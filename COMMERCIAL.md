# Commercial use

Sentinel BigEye is **free for non-commercial use** under the
[PolyForm Noncommercial License 1.0.0](LICENSE): personal use, hobby projects, study, research, schools, charities
and public bodies may use, change and share it as they like.

**Any commercial use needs a separate commercial licence** from Sentinel Armory. That includes:

- selling BigEye, or a product or device that contains or depends on it
- offering it, or something built on it, as a paid service or as part of one
- using it inside a business

A commercial licence is a written agreement with terms that fit the use: a royalty, a fixed fee or something else.
To ask for one, open an issue titled **"Commercial licence"** in this repository on GitHub (or contact the
[Sentinel Armory](https://github.com/sentinelarmory) organisation) and say what you want to build. Nothing here
grants commercial rights by itself.

Third-party parts keep their own licences (see the README); BigEye's default setup uses only parts that allow
commercial use, so a commercial licence from us covers what you need from BigEye itself.
